# What is KPI Monitoring?

<v-clicks>

- A Key Performance Indicator (KPI) is a way to measure how well a service is doing in achieving its goals

- It's like a report card that shows how well you are progressing towards your objectives - not to compare you to others

- KPIs help you to track your progress and make informed decisions
    - Are we on track to meet our goals?
    - What areas do we need to improve?

- KPIs are different for every type of service depending on what is important to measure

</v-clicks>

<v-click>

$\Rightarrow$ KPIs are relative measures! Need to be compared to some value

</v-click>
